# Boundary of the Finding Z formalism

The project reconstructs dilepton observables and compares prepared samples. It does not perform a precision extraction of parton distribution functions or electroweak couplings.

At a proton-proton collider, the beam directions are known but the event-by-event quark direction is not. The Collins-Soper frame defines a useful polar angle from beam and lepton momenta; its charge-signed cosine is not by itself a perfect quark-direction label.

The synthetic samples encode a Z-like mass peak and a simple spin-one-inspired angular pattern. They are not generated from a complete Standard Model matrix element and cannot support a precision claim.
