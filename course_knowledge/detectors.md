# Dilepton detector signatures and acceptance

Electrons normally produce charged tracks and electromagnetic-calorimeter showers. Muons normally produce charged tracks, limited calorimeter deposits, and signals in an outer muon system. The ee and mumu channels have different resolutions, efficiencies, triggers, and reducible backgrounds.

Lepton transverse-momentum and pseudorapidity requirements alter both the invariant-mass and angular distributions. A visible angular shape therefore combines dynamics with acceptance unless efficiency corrections or unfolding are performed. Finding Z does not perform a precision detector correction.
