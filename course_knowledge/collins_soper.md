# Collins-Soper angle

For negative- and positive-lepton light-cone components

\[
p_i^{\pm}=\frac{E_i\pm p_{z,i}}{\sqrt{2}},
\]

the charge-signed Collins-Soper cosine used by the tool is

\[
\cos\theta_{\mathrm{CS}}=
\frac{2\left(p_-^+p_+^- - p_-^-p_+^+\right)}
{m_{\ell\ell}\sqrt{m_{\ell\ell}^2+p_{T,\ell\ell}^2}}.
\]

Spin-one neutral-current production produces characteristic angular structure. Acceptance can distort it. At a pp collider, interpreting “forward” relative to the quark requires an additional probabilistic convention, often related to the dilepton boost and parton distributions.
