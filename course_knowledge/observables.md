# Drell-Yan observables, signal, and background

Neutral-current Drell-Yan production is the hadronic process

\[
q\bar q\to\gamma^*/Z\to\ell^+\ell^-.
\]

A resonance appears statistically as structure in the dilepton invariant-mass distribution. An event in the Z window is not individually known to come from the Z amplitude, and real neutral-current production contains photon exchange, Z exchange, and interference.

The expected selected yield has the schematic form

\[
N=\mathcal L_{\mathrm{int}}\,\sigma\,A\varepsilon.
\]

Simulation predicts distributions under model and detector assumptions; collision data are observations. The starter “signal” and “background” samples are synthetic pedagogical components, not experimentally accessible truth labels.
