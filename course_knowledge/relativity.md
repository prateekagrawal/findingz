# Relativity and dilepton reconstruction

For each reconstructed lepton, transverse momentum, pseudorapidity, azimuth, and an assumed lepton mass define a four-momentum. Four-momentum is additive:

\[
p_{\ell\ell}^{\mu}=p_{\ell^-}^{\mu}+p_{\ell^+}^{\mu}.
\]

The dilepton invariant mass is

\[
m_{\ell\ell}^2=p_{\ell\ell}^{\mu}p_{\ell\ell\mu}
=E_{\ell\ell}^2-|\mathbf p_{\ell\ell}|^2.
\]

It is a Lorentz scalar when every four-vector is transformed consistently. Dilepton transverse momentum is invariant under longitudinal boosts but not under arbitrary boosts; rapidity shifts additively under a longitudinal boost.
